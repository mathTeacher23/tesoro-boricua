Ingredients:
* Medium or Long Grain Rice: 2 Cups
* Vegetable/Olive Oil: 2.5 Tbspl
* Ham or meat: 2 o z
* Sofrito: 2 oz
* Olives & Pimiento: 1 Cup
* Tomato Sauce: 1/4 Cup
* Sazon c o n Azafran: 1 Packet
* Sazon Pork: 1 Packet
* Bay Leaf: 1
* Garlic Powder: 1 Tsp
* Black Pepper: 1/2 Tsp
* Salt: 3/4 Tsp
* Gandules (Pigeon Peas): 1 Can
* Water: 1 Cup / 1 Cup Rice

Instructions:
1. Wash and Rinse the rice until the water runs clean.
2. Set the caldero heat to medium-high, add the Oil, add the ham or meat (this will flavor the rice). You will cook the meat until it is golden brown. Add the sofrito, olives and pimiento, the tomato sauce, sazon con azafran, Sazon Pork, the bay leaf, garlic powder, black pepper, salt, and gandules.
3. Add the rinsed rice to the ingredients for about 2 minutes.
4. Then add water little by little, set the heat to medium-low, cover and cook for about 25 minutes.
5. You will leave it tightly covered and can add either aluminum foil or banana leaves to force the rice to absorb the water and flavor.
6. After the 25 minutes, mix it and if it’s still too watery then cover and check every 5 minutes until it looks ready.

Lessons Learned:
* Let the rice cook without the cover longer.
* Don't stir too much.
* Once the water is gone from the surface, then put the lid with aluminum foil to trap the heat
* Less olives. Use 1 can for every 4 cups of rice.
* Use more oil on the bottom of the caldero.
