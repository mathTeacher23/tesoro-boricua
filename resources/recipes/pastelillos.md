Ingredients:
Beef Mixture (Picadillo)
* Ground Beef: 3.5 lbs
* Salt: 2 Tbsp
* Onion Powder: 1 Tsp
* Oregano: 1 Tsp
* Garlic Salt: 3 Tbsp
* Chili Powder (optional): 2 Tbsp

Oil Mixture:
* Olive Oil: 5 Tbsp
* Sazon con Azafran: 4 packets
* Minced Garlic: 2 Tsp
* Olives (pitted): 3 Tbsp
* Cilantro or Goya Recaito (Green Jar- Culantro): 2 Tbsp
* Sofrito or Goya Sofrito (Red Jar - Tomato): 2 Tbsp
* Beef Bouillon Cubes/Packets: 3
* Tomato Paste: 2 cans
* Sugar/Sugar Substitute (optional): To taste

Dough: 
(makes about 10 6 inch discs)
* Flour (All Purpose): 2 Cups
* Water: 1 Cup
* Egg: 1
* Salt: 1 Tbsp
* Baking Powder (optional):
* Butter (or oil): 1/2 Stick

Instructions:
1. Get two large frying pans and start to make both the beef and oil mixture at the same time a this will be our Picadillo.
2. For the beef mixture, start by adding the dry ingredients to the ground beef. On Medium heat, cook the beef with the seasoning until there is no more pink. Once fully cooked, you'll drain the fat using a strainer and set the beef aside until the oil mixture is ready.
3. For the oil mixture, start by adding Olive Oil, sazon con azafran packets, minced garlic, olives, cilantro, sofrito, beef bouillon cubes and tomato paste. This you'll want high heat and mix it well. Once everything is nice and hot and blended well, turn off the heat and set it aside.
4. Add the beef back to the pan and pour the oil mixture over it. Mix it well. If it looks too dry, add more oil. Once you have it the consistency you want, taste it. If its too salty, add sugar or sugar substitute to balance the salty flavor (Equal or Splenda work best)
5. Set the meat aside and let it cool because it will need to be cool to start making the pastelillos.
6. If you want to make the dough from scratch follow this step otherwise use Goya Discos. Start by combining flour, salt and/or baking powder until combined well. Then add chilled butter or oil, and mix it with your hands and make an opening in the middle of the bowl to add the egg, and slowly add chilled water as you mix it. (2 cups of flour per 1 cup water). If it’s too sticky add more flour. If its too dry add more water. Form a ball and set it aside, covered with saran wrap in the fridge for half an hour to an hour. After the hour, roll the dough very thin and use something to cut circles about 4" in diameter. Sprinkle each cutout with flour and set aside.
7. Now we can start making the pastelillos. Add your meat (cooled) about an inch in diameter to the center of the dough. Take your finger, dip it in water and run it along the edges of the dough, fold the dough in half and use a fork to seal it shut. Set them aside to bake or fry.
8. You can cook these by either frying or baking. If you bake them, rub some melted butter to the outside of the dough. If you fry them, use cooking oil and make sure the temperature is medium heat. If you're cooking them after they've been in the refrigerator you will want it on medium heat so you don't burn the dough and leave the inside cold.
