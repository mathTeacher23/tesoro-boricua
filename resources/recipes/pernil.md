Ingredients:
* Pork Shoulder Picnic (Approximately 11 lbs)
* Pepper
* Salt
* Garlic Powder (or fresh garlic cloves
* Onion Powder
* Chile Powder (if you want)
* Cumin (if you want)
* Oregano (Fresh is better)
* Vinegar or Orange Juice (to break down the meat)
* Vegetable Oil o r Olive Oil

Instructions:
1. Clean the pork shoulder with cold water and pat it dry.
2. Get out the cutting board and make deep holes all around pork and peel/cut back the skin with a knife to make a flap or cut it off all the way if you want. Then you want to make diagonal cuts along the skin so it’s easier to cut once it’s crispy.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine all the dry ingredients (and sofrito if you want) and give the pork a bath and fill in all the holes. If you use fresh garlic do 1.5 cloves of garlic per pound of pernil ~17 cloves for an 11 lb pernil.
4. Let it marinate over night in the fridge (skin side down or skin up with salt) and cover it with plastic wrap. Best to let it marinate 24 hours and two days max (because pork can go bad quickly).
5. Once you take it out of the fridge, pat the skin dry, place it in the oven for 3 - 3.5 hours at 350 degrees tightly sealed with aluminum foil.
6. Then take out the pernil, remove the aluminum foil and place it back in the oven for about 1.5 - 2 more hours. If the skin isn't ready, you can put the oven on broil for about 5-10 minutes.

Note: 
The Pernil should not need a knife if cooking for this long. You should be able to pull it apart with tongs. The rule of thumb is to cook 30 minutes per pound of pernil.
