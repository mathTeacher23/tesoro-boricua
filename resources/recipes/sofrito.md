Sofrito
(Quantity: 2 Cups)

Ingredients:
* Onions: 2 medium sized
* Garlic Cloves: 8
* Green Pepper: 1
* Red Pepper: 1
* Roman Tomato: 1
* Sweet Peppers: 1 cup
* Recao/Culantro: 1 bunch
* Cilantro: 1 bunch
* Vinegar: 2 Tbsp
* Oregano: 1 Tspl

Instructions:
Add it all to a blender or food processor to make it nice and smooth. Since this is a base for most cooking, you want to keep it in the fridge (no more than 2 weeks) or freeze it in proportions (you can use ice trays for easy portion control).

Note:
Sofrito comes from Spain and means to fry something lightly. The origin of this recipe is unknown, but the idea is to chop the ingredients so small and cook it before we make the food we're making.
