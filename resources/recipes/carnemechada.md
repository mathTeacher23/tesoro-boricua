Carne Mechada

Ingredients:
* Rump Roast (bottom or top round - 4 pounds)
* Green Pepper (2 Large)
* Yellow Onion (2 Large)
* Jamon Packets (8)
* Tomato Paste (2 - 8oz cans)
* Olives (4 tbsp)

Optional:
* White Rice
* Carrots
* Potatoes

Instructions:
1. Take the rump roast and brown on all sides on medium heat. Once browned on all sides, take the meat out and let it rest on a cutting board
2. Create the sofrito by combining the green peppers, onions, olives, tomato paste, and jamon packets in a blender. You will need to do this little by little because this will make a lot.
3. Cut the meat into very thin slices or even 1-inch cubes - depending if you want it more of a guisada. 
    1. If making carne guisada - cut the meat into 1-inch cubes and add carrots and potatoes chopped
    2. If making carne mechada - cut the meat into half an inch thick slices (start by cutting the roast into quarters and then slices)
4. Add the sofrito to the caldera, then add the meat, and add water until the meat is fully covered and add carrots and potatoes if you want
5. Cook on low flame until tender (2-3 hours)
    1. Note: Make sure to watch the meat that the sofrito doesn’t evaporate too much. 
6. If you’re making white rice, leave about 40 minutes to cook before serving the meat.
